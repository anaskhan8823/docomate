Why Docket

Document control goes wrong in three places

Docket is built around three failures that cost project teams time and, later, arguments.

Nobody is sure which revision is current

Every document number has one current revision. Issuing a new one supersedes the old one automatically, and the old one stays in the record, clearly marked.

Nobody can prove who received what

Transmittals record the recipient, the purpose and a reply date. The review code, from A to D, is recorded against the reply, with who gave it and when.

Answers live in people's heads

Ask searches the register, mail, RFIs and transmittals together, and shows the document, revision and page it read.

How it works

From upload to answer in four steps

The same four steps apply to a drawing, a specification, a letter or a method statement.

1

Upload

Drop in a file. Docket reads the text and files it under a document number, with a discipline, type and status.

2

Control

A new revision supersedes the old one. Status codes show whether a document is for construction, approval or information.

3

Issue

Send documents by transmittal or mail. Replies and review codes are recorded, and overdue items surface on the dashboard.

4

Ask and audit

Ask the project a question. Every view, download and reply lands in the audit trail.

Safe answers

Four checks run before any answer is shown

A question is narrowed by project, role and revision before anything is ranked. These are the real passage counts for the sample project, and they change with the role you choose.

Passages left after each check
Ranking happens last, over only what remains. Every statement in an answer links to its document, revision and page.

See exactly what changed between revisions

Upload a new revision and Docket supersedes the old one, then shows the difference word by word.

  • Old revisions stay in the register, marked superseded.
  • Answers never use them, and flag when they would have matched.
  • The audit trail records who superseded what, and when.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Is this a finished product?

No. It is a concept demo with fictional projects, built to show how the product would work. Nothing in it is a real project record.

Does my uploaded file leave my browser?

No. In this demo the file is read inside your browser and held in memory until you refresh the page. Nothing is sent to a server.

Which files can Ask read?

.txt, .md, .csv, .json and .docx files. PDFs and drawings can be uploaded and downloaded, but their text is not read yet. Add a description when you upload to make them searchable.

How does Ask avoid using an old drawing?

Superseded revisions are filtered out before anything is ranked. If an old revision would have matched, the answer says so and shows what it said.

Can it replace a platform like Aconex?

It covers the daily core of document control: the register, transmittals, RFIs, mail and the audit trail. Large programmes may also need drawing viewers and model coordination, which are on the roadmap and not in the demo.

What changes when I switch role?

The register, mail, dashboard numbers, audit trail and Ask results all narrow to what that role can open.
